# Architecture Review Board (ARB) Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-EA-001
**Owner:** IT Architecture Team Leader
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective:** 2026-02-07

---

## 1. Submission
- Requestor completes the appropriate template (NAR, Conceptual Design, or Solution Design)
- Submits to the relevant Portfolio Architect for pre-review
- Portfolio Architect validates completeness and provides initial assessment

## 2. Pre-Review (Portfolio Architect)
- Assess strategic alignment with domain roadmap
- Check for application portfolio overlap
- Validate security and compliance considerations
- Prepare recommendation for ARB

## 3. ARB Scheduling
- Items added to next available ARB agenda
- Critical/urgent items may request ad-hoc session via Team Leader
- Presenter notified of scheduled slot and expected attendees

## 4. ARB Presentation
- Presenter walks through key artefacts (15-30 min)
- Q&A and discussion with board members
- Voting and decision recorded

## 5. Post-Decision
- Decision documented in meeting minutes (`Working/Meeting_Minutes/`)
- ADR created if architectural decision was made
- Requestor notified of outcome and any conditions
- Follow-up actions tracked to completion

## 6. Exception Handling
- Exceptions to architectural standards require formal exception request
- Must include: justification, risk assessment, and sunset/remediation plan
- Approved exceptions are time-bound and reviewed at expiry
