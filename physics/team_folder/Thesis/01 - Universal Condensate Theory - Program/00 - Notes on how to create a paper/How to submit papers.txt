How You Submit Papers for physics journals:

You submit:
✔ The compiled PDF
✔ The .tex file(s)
✔ The .bib file
✔ Any figures (PDF/PNG)

You do NOT submit:
❌ Word documents
❌ Raw LaTeX pasted into Word
❌ Screenshots of formulas
❌ Hand-converted PDFs


=========================================
🎯 Summary
To use MiKTeX:

Install MiKTeX
Install a LaTeX editor
Write .tex files in the editor
Click compile → MiKTeX generates a PDF
Submit the PDF to journals

You never put LaTeX → Word → PDF.
You only put LaTeX → MiKTeX → PDF.

=========================================
PREP FOR SUBMISSIONS:
-----------------------------------------

What to edit next

Author block: fill in \affiliation{} and \email{}.
Content: replace placeholders in sections/*.tex with your derivations for G=G(K,ρ,c)G=\mathcal{G}(K,\rho,c)G=G(K,ρ,c), your stability analysis, PPN/GW constraints, and cosmological fits.
Figures: drop PDFs/PNGs into figures/ and include with \includegraphics{figures/your_figure}.
References: add your sources to bib/references.bib and cite with \cite{key}.

========================================
SUBMIT:
----------------------------------------

arXiv + PRD tips

arXiv: Upload all .tex, .bib, and figures; choose TeX → pdfLaTeX.
PRD submission: Upload the compiled PDF for review; upon acceptance they’ll want the source as a ZIP/TAR (this tree is ready).
Keep math out of section/figure titles or wrap with \texorpdfstring{…}{…} to avoid bookmark warnings.
