In quantum physics, a Bose-Einstein Condensate (BEC) is a unique state of matter where a "macroscopic" number of particles behave as a single entity.1 The concept of a large fraction of bosons occupying the same ground state is the defining feature of this phase.2+1To understand why this happens, we have to look at how bosons differ from other particles and how they behave as they lose energy.1. The Bosonic "Open Door" PolicyUnlike electrons (which are fermions), bosons have an integer spin.3 This means they do not obey the Pauli Exclusion Principle.Fermions are "antisocial": only one particle can occupy a specific quantum state at a time.4Bosons are "gregarious": any number of them can occupy the same quantum state simultaneously. There is no limit.2. The Statistical "Clog"The population of bosons in any given energy state $\epsilon_i$ is governed by the Bose-Einstein distribution:$$\langle n_i \rangle = \frac{1}{e^{(\epsilon_i - \mu) / k_B T} - 1}$$As the temperature ($T$) drops, the particles lose kinetic energy and move toward lower energy levels. In a normal gas, particles spread out across many low-energy "excited" states. However, in a BEC, something dramatic happens:The excited states have a maximum capacity for how many particles they can hold at a given temperature.Once the temperature falls below a critical temperature ($T_c$), the excited states "overflow."Since the particles must go somewhere, all the "excess" bosons are forced into the single lowest-energy state available: the ground state.3. Wavefunction OverlapAs the temperature approaches absolute zero, the thermal de Broglie wavelength of the atoms (the "fuzziness" or wave-like size of the particle) grows.5At high temperatures, the wavelengths are small, and atoms act like distinct billiard balls.At $T_c$, the wavelengths become so large that they begin to overlap.When they overlap, the individual identities of the atoms vanish.6 They merge into a single, coherent quantum wave—a "super-atom"—where a large fraction of the total population (7$N$) exists in that one ground state.8+14.
